Biography

Lighting was never a career I chose — it was one I grew up inside. Raised in the concert industry by his father, James Matlock spent his formative years in the wings, watching how light shapes a room before he ever touched a board. That background gave him something most designers take years to develop: an instinct for how light feels to an audience, not just how it looks from the booth.

In high school, a stage crew class turned that instinct into craft. By the time James was designing full productions on his own, he had already built a reputation for working clean, moving fast, and delivering designs that punch above their rig budget. His credits now span high school theatre, regional professional houses, and live events — from self-designed shows on an ETC Element 1 to serving as Associate LD to David O. Smith at the Eccles Theatre for the Utah High School Musical Theatre Awards, and programming under LD James Padilla at Salt Lake Acting Company.

Currently pursuing a B.A. in Theatre Arts with a Concentration in Design and Technical Management at Weber State University on a full-tuition theatre scholarship, James brings both academic rigor and real professional experience to every project. He is a proud member of IATSE Local 99 and holds certifications in both ProTools 110 and 210M.
